# G — Randomized scenarios (round 5)

Harness: `backend/tests/test_random_scenarios.py`. Default run on the working tree: 39 passed, 2 skipped, 3 xfailed.

## What was added to the harness
- Holder pledges and releases (`pledge` step; sellable < quantity in the demat). The oracle now separates TradeLab's pending units (`pend_tl`) from hers, and a holder's delivery sale takes only her free sellable units (`her_sellable`), never pledged or settling ones.
- Holder delivery buys (`holder` / `buy` on an ETF), which show in today's positions and settle T+1.
- "No Data" positions: per-account `flat_nodata` hides rows of closed contracts, so an all-flat account gets stCode 5203.
- `blackout` step: Kotak's order book stays unreadable until the next trading day, and the next orders' replies are lost (placed or not placed). This makes old unknown entries and closes span days. `heal` ends it on the last day. New events are drawn from a separate random stream, so the older part of every seed is unchanged.
- Oracle exemptions (documented in the code): records are not judged while an account's book is down; RECORDS_TOO_HIGH is not judged for a key whose order was placed during a blackout (HANDOFF's accepted "final state never read before the book cleared"); RECORDS_TOO_HIGH on a hidden flat row of a contract TradeLab traded today (by design, `_traded_today`: kept until the next day, nothing is sent).
- New mutants `pledged_as_free`, `nodata_is_evidence`. `TL_FUZZ_BUDGET` sets the shrink budget. DONE_BUT_OPEN now prints the order rows.

## Findings
1. **R5-G1 (fixed upstream, F5-1)**: an old unknown delivery buy was finished as "nothing left" while its units were in the T+1 gap, and Sell called the run done with units left (default seed 18; ~12 seeds). Tests now regular: `test_R5_a_...` and `test_default_seeds[18]`.
2. **R5-G4 (CONFIRMED, medium-high, deterministic)**: hedge naked. Kotak's order book is unreadable all day. A Sell's buy-back of the sold PE is rejected at Kotak (the reply was fine, the rejection only shows in the book). At 23:10 the panic button, still with an unreadable book, sells the CE hedge: the PE short stays open and unhedged (shrunk from seed 880261: 5 steps, one account). Test: `test_R5_d_the_panic_button_never_sells_a_hedge_beside_a_buy_back_nobody_could_confirm` (strict xfail). Cause: a close row that is "success" with no fill read counts as a done buy-back, so wave 2 releases the hedge. Smallest fix: in the hedge wave rule (`_hedges_to_wait_for` / the panic and Exit planning), release a hedge only when the sold leg's buy-back is confirmed filled by the book or positions (row final with filled == quantity), not merely "success"; with the book unreadable, hold the hedge ("Not sold: the sold leg's buy-back is not confirmed").
3. **R5-G3 (CONFIRMED, medium, deterministic)**: after a panic buy-back that filled only part (130 of 390) while the book showed "0 of 390 filled so far", the next Sell said "F&O holds nothing bought by TradeLab" with 0 orders and the run done, but a TradeLab short of 260 was still open at Kotak (records zero). Test `test_R5_c_a_part_filled_buy_back_never_lets_sell_call_a_short_closed` (strict xfail, shrunk from seed 32500; NOECHO spec, 1 account, panic at the same time as a true-up). Suspect: the "Still working" failed row finishes the short's record as closed. Smallest fix: records of a sold leg must not be brought to zero by a buy-back row whose fill is unknown or partial; keep the remainder until positions or the book show it flat. Needs a closer look by the fixer.
4. **R5-G2 (CONFIRMED, low-medium, deterministic, touches the holder's own lots)**: HANDOFF's accepted case plus a coincidence. The book is unreadable all day; a Buy's order (part-filled 0 lots) keeps its full quantity as a record. The next day Kotak lists the holder's own long of the same size on that contract, so Sell sells it (130 units of her CE). Test `test_R5_b_an_order_whose_fill_was_never_read_does_not_make_sell_close_the_holders_own_lots` (strict xfail, seed 30949). Fix: a non-final "success" row from an earlier day whose fill was never read must not be sized against Kotak's figure when the holder may own the same contract; report it and send nothing (as for unknown rows).
5. Seeds 31036, 31677, 34135 (CNC, RECORDS_TOO_LOW 1 to 12 units, holder sells her own unit during the settling gap): the shrunk case lacks the 15:40 true-up that notes her units, so it is HANDOFF's accepted "true-up didn't run" case. 31677 and 34135 were not shrunk: OPEN (probably the same). Seed 33607 (DONE_BUT_OPEN 100 units): the shrunk spec lost its overnight step, an artifact; the original was not re-checked: OPEN.
6. STOPLOSS_FLIP (30401, 30509): designed (C3: report, never cancel her orders). No other new violation kinds. No wrong order was found on pledges: no `pledged_as_free` check was run (see below).
